Train Smart

IRONPATH provides general fitness information for educational purposes. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional.

  • Consult a physician before starting this or any exercise program — especially if you have a pre-existing condition, injury, or haven't trained in a long time.
  • Bodyweight strength training is demanding. Progress at your own pace; never train through sharp or joint pain.
  • Check that bars, rings, chairs, and other equipment are stable and rated for your weight before loading them.
